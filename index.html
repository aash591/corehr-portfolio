As a general rule, EPF registration applies to establishments employing 20 or more people, and ESI to establishments employing 10 or more (a few states set that threshold at 20), covering employees who earn up to ₹21,000 a month in gross wages. Thresholds, wage ceilings and the way employee count is worked out differ by state, sector and establishment type — and voluntary registration is possible below the threshold. We confirm what applies to your specific facts before anything is filed.

Both PF and ESI contributions are currently due by the 15th of the month following the wage month — so April wages are paid over by 15 May. Missing a due date attracts interest and damages under the respective schemes, which is exactly why we work backwards from those dates when setting your payroll calendar.

PF is currently 12% from the employee and 12% from the employer on basic wages plus dearness allowance, with part of the employer share going to the pension scheme. ESI is currently 0.75% from the employee and 3.25% from the employer on gross wages. Rates are periodically revised by the government, and how they apply depends on your salary structure — we compute and reconcile these as part of the payroll run.

LWF is a state-level welfare contribution — not every state operates one, and those that do set their own contribution amounts, employee thresholds and filing cycles, which may be monthly, half-yearly or annual. Kerala operates a Labour Welfare Fund. We check applicability for each state you employ people in, rather than assuming it applies everywhere.
